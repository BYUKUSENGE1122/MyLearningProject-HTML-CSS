Essential Digital Tourism Information

Navigating the capital like a local requires utilizing its tech features and respecting its environment-first laws.

Smart City Parameter How It Benefits Your Visit
Cashless Economy (Momo) Virtually every hotel, street vendor, and taxi accepts digital payments via Mobile Money (MoMo) or card taps. Carrying thick wads of cash is unnecessary.
Strict Anti-Plastic Laws Non-biodegradable plastic bags are strictly prohibited across Rwanda. Do not pack items in plastic baggies; your luggage will be inspected at Kigali International Airport.
Umuganda Day Participation On the last Saturday morning of every month, the city pauses for community cleanup and public works. Stores close temporarily as locals work together to keep the streets pristine.
Safety Standard Kigali is proudly ranked among the safest cities in the world. Walking solo through well-lit pedestrian zones at midnight is perfectly safe and normal.
